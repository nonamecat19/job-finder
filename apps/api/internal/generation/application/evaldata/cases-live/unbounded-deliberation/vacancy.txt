Senior Machine Learning Engineer — Inference Platform

done this before at scale. You will own the inference path end to end: kernel-level
performance work, distributed serving, and the platform the research team deploys onto.

Required:
- Strong Python and C++; comfortable in CUDA
- Deep experience with PyTorch and production model serving
- Distributed systems and Kubernetes in production
- A track record of measurable latency or throughput improvements

Nice to have:
- JAX or Triton
- Model compression, quantization or pruning
- Published research in efficient inference
- Experience scaling an engineering team

You will work with a small senior team and are expected to make architectural decisions
independently.
